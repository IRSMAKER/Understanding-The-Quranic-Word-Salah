
## A2 - 4:30 - fa-sawfa nuslihi naran

ARABIC (flow): 4:29 "O you who believe, do not consume one another's wealth unjustly, except through trade by mutual consent, and do not kill yourselves. Indeed God is Merciful to you" -> **4:30 "And whoever does that in aggression and injustice - We will ṣ-l-w him with a Fire, and that was easy for God"** -> 4:31 "If you avoid the major sins which you are forbidden, We will remove from you your lesser sins and admit you to a noble entrance" -> 4:55 "And some of them believed in it and some of them turned away from it, and sufficient is Jahannam as a Sa'ir" -> 4:56 "Indeed those who disbelieved in Our signs - We will ṣ-l-w them with a Fire..." (A3).

WORD FORM: fa-sawfa nuslihi naran - "We will ṣ-l-w him with a Fire" - future tense, divine "We", person + fire as TWO objects (double-object grammar: nuslihi naran = "We ṣ-l-w him [with] a Fire"). The person and the fire are BOTH objects of the verb - the fire is the instrument/medium of the ṣ-l-w.

CONTEXT: MADANI, Surah an-Nisa' (4). The verse sits in the property-and-life passage (4:29-31): do not consume wealth unjustly, do not kill yourselves (4:29); whoever does that in aggression and injustice - We will ṣ-l-w him with a Fire (4:30); if you avoid major sins, We will remove lesser sins and admit you to a noble entrance (4:31). Then later (4:55-56): some believed, some turned away; Jahannam as Sa'ir; We will ṣ-l-w them with a Fire.

GRAMMAR & LEXICON:
- DOUBLE-OBJECT GRAMMAR: nuslihi naran - the person (him) and the fire (a Fire) are BOTH objects of the verb. The fire is the INSTRUMENT/MEDIUM of the ṣ-l-w: "We will ṣ-l-w him WITH a Fire." The ṣ-l-w is performed WITH the fire - the fire is the tool of the standing-delivery. This matches A1's "swallow fire inside the belly" - the fire is the medium of the ṣ-l-w.
- "AND THAT WAS EASY FOR GOD" (wa-kāna dhālika ʿalā Allāhi yasīran): the ṣ-l-w-delivery is EASY for God. The same ease-formula as 74:17 ("I will burden him with climbing"). The ṣ-l-w is not a difficult act for God; it is His easy act. The standing-delivery is effortless for the Deliverer.
- BREACH-CONTEXTS: the verse is preceded by 4:29's two prohibitions: (1) do not consume wealth unjustly (property), (2) do not kill yourselves (life). The ṣ-l-w is the consequence of violating BOTH property and life rights. The ṣ-l-w is the penalty for injustice toward people's wealth and life. This matches 4:10's (A1) "those who consume the property of orphans unjustly... swallow fire."
- 4:55 "Jahannam as Sa'ir" - the fire-lexicon of the Quran is one coherent system: Jahannam is named as the Sa'ir (blazing). The ṢAL-W is performed WITH the fire; the fire has NAMES (Jahannam, Sa'ir, Jahim, Saqar, Sa'ir). The fire-lexicon is unified.
- "WHOEVER DOES THAT" (man yafʿalu dhālika): the ṣ-l-w is for "whoever does that" - the ṣ-l-w is for the DOER, not the believer. The ṣ-l-w is for the one who ACTS unjustly.

PARALLELS:
- A1 (4:10): A1's "those who consume the property of orphans unjustly... swallow fire" and A2's "whoever does that in aggression and injustice - We will ṣ-l-w him with a Fire" are the SAME breach-context (property-injustice) with the SAME fire-consequence. The ṣ-l-w is the penalty for property-injustice.
- A3 (4:56 - skin-roasting): A3's skin-roasting detail (total contact) will confirm that the ṣ-l-w is TOTAL CONTACT with the fire. A2's "with a Fire" (naran) is the medium; A3's "their skins are roasted" is the effect.
- A5 (14:29 - "Jahannam yaslawnaha"): A5's "they will be ṣ-l-w-ed with her" uses the same verb-object structure as A2's "We will ṣ-l-w him with a Fire." The person is ṣ-l-w-ed WITH the fire.
- 4:31 "If you avoid the major sins... We will remove from you your lesser sins and admit you to a noble entrance" - the POSITIVE counterpart: avoid major sins -> noble entrance. The ṣ-l-w (fire) is for the wrongdoer; the noble entrance is for the avoider. The same standing-state, two directions.

FINDING: A2 confirms the Ṣ-L-W root's fire-side: "We will ṣ-l-w him with a Fire" - the fire is the MEDIUM of the ṣ-l-w. The ṣ-l-w is performed WITH the fire. The fire is not just a punishment; it is the MEDIUM of a standing-state: the person is ṣ-l-w-ed WITH the fire, meaning he is joined into and lodged within the fire's reach. The double-object grammar (person + fire) shows that the ṣ-l-w is a RELATION between the person and the fire: the person is delivered into the fire's reach. And "that was easy for God" shows that the ṣ-l-w is effortless for the Deliverer - the standing-delivery is His easy act.
