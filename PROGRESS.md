# PROGRESS — Understanding the Quranic Word "Salah" (صلاة)

## Goal
Determine the true Quranic meaning of the word **Salah** by studying every verse in which its root (Ṣ-L-W / ص-ل-و) occurs, verse by verse, against the overall Quranic context — ending in a comprehensive research document.

## Corpus & Method
- Source: `quran-simple-min.txt` (Tanzil Simple-Minimal, 6236 verses, UTF-8, partial diacritics) + `quran-simple-min.sql` (same data).
- Environment has no Python/SQLite/Node, so **PowerShell 5.1 is used as the query engine**. Console display mangles Arabic, so the workflow is always:
  1. **Scripts are pure ASCII** (PowerShell 5.1 mis-reads non-ASCII scripts as ANSI).
  2. Arabic patterns live in **UTF-8 data files** (`family_words.txt`, `drop_verses.txt`).
  3. Query results are written to UTF-8 files and read back with the `read` tool.
- `quran-clean.txt` — diacritics-stripped copy built for reliable matching (script: `_build_clean.ps1`).

## Emerging thesis (live draft for the final doc)
THE S-L-W CORE: to be JOINED INTO and LODGED WITHIN the full working reach of a power that determines one - sustained, renewed, settled exposure; "assigned lodging" proven three ways: (1) settlement-vocabulary (qarar / masir / mihad / maab); (2) ja'alna lahu jahannam (assigned-to-him grammar); (3) awla biha (worthiness-of-possession grammar).
THREE STABLE POLES: SITE (space assigned to standing with the Name: salawat 22:40, musalla 2:125) - STATE (assigned standing kept up willingly: the noun salah, B/C/D/E) - DELIVERY (assigned standing imposed: the fire verses A).
FOUR VOICES OF THE FIRE-VERB ALL ATTESTED: future (yaslawnaha), divine act (nusliehi), earned title (saliyyan), self-command (asluwha) - one settled standing in every voice, keyed to covenant-response.
A12 FORMAL GIFT: the root acts-noun (Tasliya, same morphology pattern as Tasbih) APPEARS IN FIRE USAGE - "a serving of sustained roasting" - so the nouns of the root name a KEPT-UP STANDING-STATE in BOTH directions (Tasliya 56:94, Salwah 69:31 vs Salah/salawat/musalla). Plus hospitality grammar (nazl + tasliya = a served bill) and the nearness/sight contrast (56:85).
Honor economy: madhmum/madhur (A6) vs divine honoring. Surah-bridges now THREE: Surah 19 (19:59 lost salah -> 19:70 saliyyan), Surah 74 (74:26 fire-assignment + 74:43 "we were NOT of the musallin"), Surah 87 (87:12 the-greatest-fire vs 87:15 fa-salla the God-side stand).
A17-A18-A20-A22 PASSIVE-SET: four near-passive fire-forms (84:12 / 87:12 / 88:4 / 111:3) - the fire-stand is a state DONE TO THE PERSON; A21's exclusion-grammar (la-yasla-ha illa-l-ashqa) + 92:17 keep-away grammar: the fire-club is a confined class and BOTH keepings are God's acts.
A22 NAME-TAG: the fire-stand is name-signed (Abu-Lahab's own kunya names the fire that will hold him) - standing is name-marked in both directions: fa-salla in the Mentioned Name (87:15) vs fire-of-LAHAB in his own name (111:3).
A-CLUSTER CLOSED (22/22): s-l-w = a standing-state within the reach of a power; taken VOLUNTARILY toward the mentioned Name (fa-salla) or INSTALLED via the fire (y-u-s-la) after one's own deny-and-turn (4:115 / 58:8 / 92:16) and wealth-failure (A1 A14 A17 A21 A22 thread).
B-CLUSTER POLARITY (7/7): the divine ṢALĀH is the OPPOSITE pole of the fire-stand: (1) GRANTED not imposed (2:157 ṢALAWĀT + mercy, to those who confess return); (2) EXODUS not delivery (33:43 brings OUT of darkness into light, with angels as co-agents); (3) COMMANDED (33:56 ṢALLŪ ʿALAYHI + SALLIMŪ TASLĪMAN - standing-toward + surrender); (4) CURRENCY (9:99 spending counted as ṢALAWĀT of the Messenger); (5) TRANQUILITY (9:103 "your ṣalāh is their sakan"); (6) INNATE (24:41 the birds know their ṣalāh by creation); (7) WITHHELD (9:84 "do not s-l-l on them, ever" - the honor-economy's sharpest form). The ṢALĀH-chain: human act (ṣalāh/spending/repentance) -> divine ṢALĀH (with angels) -> exodus (darkness->light) -> SALĀM-greeting (33:44). The ṢALĀH always qualified by a soft-quality (raḥmah/sakan/SALĀM/taslīm). The same stem, two directions: imposed-standing (fire) vs granted-standing (God's ṢALĀH).
C-CLUSTER (21/21): the COMMAND-SIDE's ṢALĀH: ESTABLISHED (yuqīmū - make-stand the standing-state, not performed as a momentary act) in SEVEN forms: physical (standing, C13), temporal (timed, C14), vocal (moderated voice, C15), preservative (guarded, C16), personal (commanded to the self, C19), purifying (purification-package, C20), eased (adjusted to capacity, C21). The ṢALĀH + ZAKAH pair is the UNIVERSAL formula (stable across 8+ suras). The ṢALĀH is the MINIMAL RELIGION (98:5: worship God purely + upright + establish ṢALĀH + give zakah = "religion of uprightness"). The ṢALĀH is the VEHICLE of REMEMBRANCE (20:14 "establish the ṢALĀH for My remembrance"). The ṢALĀH WIPES SIN (11:114 "good deeds remove evil deeds"). The ṢALĀH is a FOUR-FOLD DEDICATION (6:162 "my ṢALĀH... is for God" - standing + sacrifice + life + death). The SURAH-19 BRIDGE (19:59 "lost ṢALĀH + followed desires" -> 19:68 kneeling -> 19:70 ṣalāh-loss -> fire). The SITE-lexeme MUSSALLA (2:125 - the believer's standing-site, inherited from Abraham's MAQĀM).
D-CLUSTER (44/44): the LIFE-INSTITUTION side: the ṢALĀH has TWENTY-NINE additional forms (adaptive, patient, pure, washed, bowing, called, enemy-targeted, legal, fear-paired, Book-holding, peace-condition, brotherhood-condition, mosque-maintaining, laziness-targeted, community-bond, directed, God-facing, urgent, leadership, heart-trembling, first-act-of-the-empowered, no-difficulty, non-distracted, successional, household-clock, Hereafter-certain, turning-to-God, eternal-trade, response-consultative, forgiven, economic-clock, hypocrite-lazy-show-little, mocker-whistling-clapping, successional, household-clock). The ṢALĀH is the ANTI-USURY act (2:275-277), the ANTI-IDOLATRY act (6:72, 30:31), the PEACE-act (4:77, 9:5), the BROTHERHOOD-condition (9:11), the MOSQUE-MAINTENANCE act (9:18), the LEGAL anchor (5:106), the ECONOMIC clock (62:9-10), the HOUSEHOLD clock (24:58), the WASHING-dimension (5:6), the CALL-dimension (5:58, 62:9), the ADAPTABILITY (shortened in fear, tayammum, on foot or riding). The ṢALĀH is the DIVIDER between true and false belief (2:3, 8:3, 27:3, 31:4).
E-CLUSTER (13/13): the TRANSFORMATIVE check: the ṢALĀH PREVENTS from immorality and wrongdoing (29:45 "the ṢALĀH prevents from immorality and wrongdoing; the remembrance of God is greater"). The ṢALĀH is the act of the HUMBLE (23:2), the CONSTANT (70:22-23), the GUARDED (23:9, 70:34), the PREVENTIVE (29:45), and the FORGIVEN (58:13). The ṢALĀH is also the act of the HYPOCRITE (4:142 lazy/show-off/little-remember, 8:35 whistling/clapping, 107:4-7 heedless/show/withhold), the REJECTOR (75:31 neither believed nor prayed), and the PERSECUTED (96:9-10 forbidden to pray). The ṢALĀH is the DIVIDER: it either transforms (prevents evil) or condemns (hypocrite/rejector).
FIRST-PASS COMPLETE (108/108): the ṢALĀH has FORTY-FIVE (and more) forms, all "state" features. The root Ṣ-L-W = a standing-state within the reach of a power; taken VOLUNTARILY toward the Mentioned Name (fa-ṣallā) or INSTALLED via the fire (y-u-s-la). The SAME stem, TWO directions: imposed-standing (fire, DELIVERY pole) vs granted-standing (God's ṢALĀH, STATE pole) vs assigned-space (MUSSALLA/ṢALAWĀT/MIḤRĀB, SITE pole). The ṢALĀH is both a state (what you ARE) and a state (what you ARE IN) - the same standing-state in both directions.

- B6 (9:99): the ṢALĀH-as-CURRENCY - spending counted as ṢALAWĀT of the Messenger. The ṢALĀH can be EARNED (by spending, by patience 2:157, by repentance 9:103). THREE human inputs to the ṢALĀH-chain. The Messenger is the CHANNEL (B4 divine ṢALĀH on him; B6 human ṢALĀH through him).
- B7 (9:84): the NEGATIVE-command - "do NOT s-l-l on them, EVER, and do NOT stand at his grave." The ṢALĀH is HONOR that can be WITHHELD; withholding = dishonor (la'nah). The honor-economy's sharpest form. The SAME passage contains both: disbelievers denied ṢALĀH (9:84) and believers earning ṢALĀH (9:99). B-cluster complete.
- C5 (2:125): the SITE-lexeme MUSSALLA - "take from the standing-place of Abraham a MUSSALLA." The believer's standing-site is a SINGLE assigned spot, inherited from Abraham's MAQĀM. Paired with: the IMAMATE (Abraham as Imam), the COVENANT of PURIFICATION (purify My House), the SUBMISSION (aslim - the submitter stands at the MUSSALLA), the RITES (manāsik, shown not invented), the MESSENGER (recites/teaches/purifies), the UPRIGHT RELIGION (ḥanīf), and the SCROLLS (already written in Abraham's and Moses' Scrolls). - C1 (98:5): THE MINIMAL RELIGION - "worship God purely (mukhliṣīn) + upright (ḥanīfan) + ESTABLISH THE SALAH + give the zakah = the religion of uprightness (dīn al-qayyimah)." - D9-D11 (4:101-103): the ṢALĀH's ADAPTABILITY and LEGALITY - SHORTENED (qaṣr) in travel/fear (4:101), performed IN SHIFTS during war (4:102), ADAPTED to rain/sickness (4:102), and a PRESCRIBED WRIT (kitāban mawqūtan) with fixed times (4:103). The ṢALĀH is both FLEXIBLE (adapts to circumstances) and FIXED (has prescribed times). (Studied ahead of canonical order; canonical next is D1 2:3.)
