## A3 - 4:56 - sawfa nuslihim naran

ARABIC (flow): 4:55 "And some of them believed in it and some of them turned away from it, and sufficient is Jahannam as a Sa'ir" -> **4:56 "Indeed those who disbelieved in Our signs - We will ṣ-l-w them with a Fire. Every time their skins are roasted through (kullamā nadijat julūduhum), We will replace them with other skins (badalnāhum julūdan ghayrahā) so that they may TASTE (li-yadhūqū) the punishment. Indeed God is Exalted in Might and Wise"** -> 4:57 "And those who believed and did righteous deeds - We will admit them to gardens beneath which rivers flow, abiding therein eternally; for them therein are purified spouses; and We will admit them to a deep shade."

WORD FORM: sawfa nuslihim naran - "We will ṣ-l-w them with a Fire" - future tense, divine "We", them + fire as two objects (same double-object grammar as A2: nuslihim naran). The skin-roasting detail: "kullamā nadijat julūduhum badalnāhum julūdan ghayrahā li-yadhūqū al-ʿadhāba" - "every time their skins are roasted through, We will replace them with other skins so that they may taste the punishment."

CONTEXT: MADANI, Surah an-Nisa' (4). The verse sits in the disbeliever-passage (4:55-57): some believed, some turned away; Jahannam as Sa'ir (4:55); We will ṣ-l-w them with a Fire (4:56); the believers admitted to gardens (4:57). The verse is the NEGATIVE counterpart of 4:57 (believers -> gardens).

GRAMMAR & LEXICON:
- THE SKIN-ROASTING (kullamā nadijat julūduhum): "Every time their skins are roasted through" - the ṣ-l-w is TOTAL CONTACT through the skin. The skin is the organ of EXPOSURE: it is the boundary between the person and the fire. The fire does not just burn the skin; it ROASTS it through (nadija = to be roasted/cooked through). The skin is the organ of contact.
- THE REPLACEMENT (badalnāhum julūdan ghayrahā): "We will replace them with other skins" - the skin is REPLACED so that the roasting can continue. The organ of exposure is replaced so that the exposure never ends. The ṢAL-W is a SUSTAINED, RENEWED standing-state: the exposure is not a moment but a cycle - roast -> replace -> roast -> replace. This is the "renewed cycles" (ṣalawāt) of the fire-side.
- THE TASTING (li-yadhūqū al-ʿadhāba): "so that they may TASTE the punishment" - the ṢAL-W is TASTED (dhawq = tasting). The ṢAL-W is not just felt; it is TASTED - full sensory registration. The same as A20's "no food but dari; no fattening nor satisfying hunger" (88:6-7) - the fire-stand is a continuous tasting. The ṢAL-W is a STATE of continuous tasting, not a momentary burn.
- THE TOTAL CONTACT: the skin is roasted through (nadija), replaced (badal), and tasted (dhawq). The ṢAL-W is TOTAL CONTACT through the skin, with the skin replaced so the contact never ends. The ṢAL-W is a SUSTAINED, RENEWED, TOTAL standing-state: the person is joined into and lodged within the fire's reach, and the contact is never-ending.
- "INDEED GOD IS EXALTED IN MIGHT AND WISE" (ʿazīzan ḥakīman): the ṢAL-W is performed by the Mighty and Wise God. The ṢAL-W is a wise and mighty act - the same as D12 (4:162 "the firm in knowledge... establishers of the ṢALĀH") - the ṢAL-W requires might and wisdom.

PARALLELS:
- A2 (4:30 - "We will ṣ-l-w him with a Fire"): A2's "with a Fire" (naran) is the medium; A3's "their skins are roasted" is the effect. The medium (fire) and the effect (skin-roasting) are paired: the fire roasts the skin; the skin is replaced; the fire roasts again.
- A5 (14:29 - "Jahannam yaslawnaha"): A5's "they will be ṣ-l-w-ed with her" uses the same verb-object structure. The person is ṣ-l-w-ed WITH the fire. A3's skin-roasting shows what "with the fire" means: total contact.
- A20 (88:4-7 - "tusla naran hamiya... no food but dari"): A20's body-list (drink, food, hearing, furniture) confirms A3's "total contact": the fire-stand is a FULL-BODY state. A3's skin-roasting is the SKIN-level of the full-body state.
- A12 (56:94 - "tasliya" = kept-up roasting): A12's "tasliya" (kept-up roasting) is the NOUN of A3's "kullamā nadijat" (every time roasted through). The fire-ṣalāh is a KEPT-UP roasting: roast -> replace -> roast -> replace. The noun (tasliya) and the verb (nadija) describe the same cycle.
- 4:57 "those who believed... We will admit them to gardens" - the POSITIVE counterpart: the believers are admitted to gardens; the disbelievers are ṣ-l-w-ed with Fire. The same standing-state, two directions: fire-delivery vs garden-admission.

FINDING: A3 confirms that the Ṣ-L-W root's fire-side is a SUSTAINED, RENEWED, TOTAL standing-state: the skin is roasted through (nadija), replaced (badal), and tasted (dhawq) - the exposure is never-ending. The ṢAL-W is not a momentary burn but a CYCLE of exposure: roast -> replace -> roast -> replace. The skin is the organ of exposure, and it is replaced so the exposure never ends. This is the "renewed cycles" (ṣalawāt) of the fire-side: the ṢAL-W is a RENEWED standing-state, not a one-time event. And the ṢAL-W is TASTED (dhawq) - full sensory registration, not a passing sensation. The ṢAL-W is a STATE of continuous tasting, renewed through skin-replacement.

CARRIED FORWARD: (i) The SKIN-ROASTING cycle (nadija -> badal -> dhawq) - the fire-ṣalāh is a cycle of exposure. HOLD for the final doc's cycle-table. (ii) The TASTING (dhawq) - the ṢAL-W is tasted, not just felt. HOLD for the final doc's sensory-table (matches A20's body-list: drink/food/hearing/furniture/taste/skin). (iii) The REPLACEMENT (badal) - the organ of exposure is replaced so the exposure never ends. HOLD for the final doc's renewal-table (matches A12's tasliya = kept-up roasting). (iv) The TOTAL CONTACT (nadija = roasted through) - the ṢAL-W is total contact through the skin. HOLD for the final doc's contact-table (matches A1's "swallow fire inside the belly").
