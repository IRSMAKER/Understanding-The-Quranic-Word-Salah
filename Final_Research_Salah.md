# The Quranic Meaning of Ṣ-L-W (صلاة) — Comprehensive Research Document

**Prepared from:** verse-by-verse study of all 108 distinct Ṣ-L-W root verses (118 occurrences, 42 word forms) in `quran-simple-min.txt` (Tanzil Simple-Minimal, 6236 verses), with diacritics-stripped matching via `quran-clean.txt`.

**Method:** Each verse studied in its Quranic context (surrounding verses + thematic parallels), with grammar, lexicon, and cross-referencing of every parallel formula in the Quran. Look-alike roots (وصل join/reach, صلح rectify, فصل separate, صلب crucify, etc.) were excluded after reading them. Study order: thematic clusters A→E (root-language anchors → divine ṣalāh → commanded formula → life-institutions → transformative check), canonical order within each cluster.

**Files:** `1_Salah_Verses.md` (collected verses), `2_Study_Order.md` (study plan, all 108 ticked), `3_Study_Notes.md` (verse-by-verse journal, 101 entries covering 108 verses), `PROGRESS.md` (live thesis draft).

## Part 1 — The settled core (live thesis)

The root Ṣ-L-W (ص-ل-و) names a **standing-state within the full working reach of a power**. Not a momentary act — a sustained, renewed, settled exposure. Three grammatical proofs of "assigned lodging":

1. **Settlement-vocabulary** attached to the fire-usage: مَصِير (returning-settlement, 4:115), قَرَار (settled abode, 14:29), مِهَاد (bedding, 38:56), مَآب (return-hostel, 38:55). The Quran sells the fire as a HOME-address.
2. **Assigned-to-him grammar**: جَعَلْنَا لَهُ جَهَنَّمَ (17:18 "We MADE FOR HIM Jahannam") — the lodging is grammatically assigned to the person.
3. **Worthiness-of-possession grammar**: أَوْلَىٰ بِهَا صِلِيًّا (19:70 "most worthy of being ṣ-l-w-ed in her") — the fire is a property of the worthy.

The root has **three stable poles**:

| Pole | Meaning | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **SITE** | space assigned to standing with God's Name | 22:40 (ṣalawāt), 2:125 (muṣallā), 3:39 (miḥrāb) |
| **STATE** | assigned standing kept up willingly | the noun ṣalāh/ṣalawāt (B/C/D/E clusters) |
| **DELIVERY** | assigned standing, imposed | the fire verses (A-cluster) |

The **noun ṣalāh** = the STATE pole, willingly kept. The **fire-usage** = the DELIVERY pole, imposed. The **site-words** = the SITE pole. Same stem, three directions.

The root also has an **honor-economy**: مَذْمُوم/مَدْحُور (blameworthy, driven-off, A6) vs divine ṣalāh (honoring, bringing out of darkness into light, B3). And a **breach-pattern**: in every fire-usage, the delivered person first steered himself (ate the trust 4:10, defied the Messenger 4:115, followed desires 17:18/19:59, turned away 92:15) — the fire-ṣalāh is always **self-steering → self-settlement**.

## Part 2 — Cluster A: The Fire-Verses (DELIVERY pole, 22 verses)

The fire-cluster is the root's **DELIVERY pole**: ṣ-l-w naming an assigned, imposed standing-state within the reach of a power (fire/Jahannam/Saqar/Sa'īr). All four Arabic voices are attested: future (يَصْلَوْنَهَا), divine act (نُصْلِيهِ), earned title (صِلِيًّا), self-command (اُصْلُوهَا), and passive (يُصْلَىٰ).

### Key verses and findings

**A1 (4:10)** — "Those who consume the property of orphans unjustly... swallow fire in their bellies." The Ṣ-L-W root = sustained delivery-into the grip of a power. The "swallow fire inside the belly" idiom occurs only here and 2:174. Breach-context: property-injustice.

**A2 (4:30)** — "Whoever does that in aggression and injustice - We will ṣ-l-w him with a Fire, and that was easy for God." Double-object grammar (person + fire both objects); delivery is God's OWN act ("We"). Breach-contexts: injustice toward wealth/life (4:10, 4:29-30) and rejection of revelation (4:55-56). Jahannam named as the Sa'īr (4:55).

**A3 (4:56)** — "We will ṣ-l-w them with a Fire. Every time their skins are roasted through, We will replace them with other skins so that they may taste the punishment." Skin-roasting = TOTAL CONTACT through the skin; the organ of exposure is REPLACED so "tasting" never ends. The ṢAL-W is a SUSTAINED, RENEWED, TOTAL standing-state: roast -> replace -> roast -> replace. The noun (tasliya = kept-up roasting) and the verb (nadija = roasted through) describe the same cycle.

**A4 (4:115)** — "We will ṣ-l-w him with Jahannam." The PLACE itself becomes the exposure-agent. Paired with نُوَلِّهِ مَا تَوَلَّىٰ (We leave him to his own steering) - the delivery is the CULMINATION of self-steering. Breach = defying the Messenger + leaving the believers' shared course.

**A5 (14:29)** — "Jahannam - they will be ṣ-l-w-ed with her." Pronoun attaches to the fire while people are subject = "lodged INTO her residence" (confirmed by "awful a settling-place"). Deep contrast: 14:24's rooted tree (أَصْلُهَا ثَابِتٌ) vs the fire-address أَصْلُوْهَا (36:64).

**A6 (17:18)** — "We MADE FOR HIM Jahannam, that he may ṣ-l-w her, blameworthy and driven-off." Third grammatical proof of assigned lodging (جَعَلْنَا لَهُ). Delivered states = مَذْمُوم/مَدْحُور (blameworthy, expelled) - exact negative of divine ṢALĀH's honoring. Desire-tempo (triple عجل) mirrors destination-tempo.

**A7 (19:70)** — "those who are most worthy of being ṣ-l-w-ed in her" (صِلِيًّا). Earned TITLE of fire-delivery (أَوْلَىٰ بِهَا). GOLDEN BRIDGE: Surah 19 chains 19:59 "lost the ṢALĀH + followed desires" → 19:68 kneeling at fire → 19:70 ṣalāh-loss → fire-delivery. Noun and verb of one root = one standing-assignment.

**A8 (22:40)** — "ṣalawāt" (houses of worship, Jewish prayer-houses). The OUTLIER: the only Ṣ-L-W occurrence naming BUILDINGS. Criterion: "in which the name of God is mentioned much." Other communities get the plural site-word (ṣalawāt); believers get the singular assigned-spot (MUSSALLĀ, 2:125). The root's SITE-pole.

**A9 (36:64)** — "ṣ-l-w her today, for what you used to deny." IMPERATIVE voice added: the delivered one is TOLD, not dragged. Covenant frame: worship God vs deny Him. "Today" twice frames the covenant-expiry settlement.

**A10 (38:56)** — "Jahannam - they will be ṣ-l-w-ed with her; awful a BEDDING (mihād)." Settlement-lexicon grows: maṣīr/qarār/mihād/ma'āb = return/lodge/bed places. The fire is a HOME-address.

**A11 (52:16)** — "ṣ-l-w her; be patient or not, it is the same for you." Even patience is indistinguishable INSIDE the delivery. Responses belong on THIS side of it. Self-steering closure (innamā tujzawna mā kuntum taʿmalūn).

**A12 (56:94)** — "wa-taṣliyatu jahīm." FORMAL GIFT: the root's acts-noun (taṣliya, same pattern as tasbīḥ) appears in FIRE usage = "a serving of sustained roasting." The fire-stand is named with the NOUN of the root itself. Hospitality-grammar (nazl + tasliya = served bill). Nearness/sight contrast (56:85: God nearer but unseen). Purity-gate (56:79: none touch it except the purified). Three-way standing-category template (muqarrab/ashab-yamin/mukadhdhibin-dhalleen).

**A13 (58:8)** — "hasbuhum jahannam yaslawnaha; awful a maṣīr." SUFFICING-GRAMMAR: the fire serves as the person's own sufficer, recruited by his own speech-record. Constant divine presence (58:7 "He is WITH them wherever") makes the fire-delivery = the standing-state of REFUSED proximity. False-greeting (hayyūka bi-mā lam yahyika) = the anti-salam standing-state.

**A14 (69:31)** — "thumma-l jahima ṣalwahu." The BARE NOUN salwah joins the root's noun-family (taṣliya + salwah). "al-jahima salwahu" readable as noun OR command - both yield the same assigned standing. Installation-procession: seize -> shackle -> deliver-stand -> thread-through (69:32). Fault-list = belief + urging-feeding-the-poor (69:33-34).

**A15 (74:26)** — "sa-aṣliyhi saqar." God's FIRST-PERSON FUTURE assignment. Surah 74 OPENS with the standing-prescription (arise/warn/magnify/purify/abandon/no-favor-counting/patience) and CLOSES with the two-fault confession (74:43 "we were NOT of the muṣallīn" + 74:44 not feeding poor). SECOND SURAH-BRIDGE (19 & 74). Saqar is a RECRUITED POWER-SPACE (guards = angels, count = standing-gate). The s-l-k threading word (74:42) companions the s-l-w standing family.

**A16 (82:15)** — "yaslawonaha yawma-d-dīn." Locked by the NO-ABSENCE clause (82:16 "they are not absent from her"). Fire-standing is non-absentible. TWIN GUARD-FIELD: recorder-keepers (82:10-12) vs fire-guard angels (74:31). The standing-state is HELD BY KEEPERS WHO WRITE THE REAL RECORD.

**A17 (84:12)** — "wa-yuṣlā saʿīran." The ONLY FULLY-PASSIVE fire-verb: the person is pure object. The root's internal rule surfaces: "ONE WHO WILL NOT STAND WILL BE STOOD" (sujūd-refusal in the same sura, 84:21). The meet-word (84:6 "toil toward your Lord and you will MEET Him") names the positive anchor.

**A18 (87:12)** — "yasla an-nara al-kubra." Fifth fire-name (THE-GREATEST-fire). The NEITHER-LIFE-NOR-DEATH clause (87:13) proves the fire-stand is a STANDING-STATE, not an event. al-Ashqā avoids-her (same lexeme as 92:15). The recite-gate + ease-gate precede the standing-divide.

**A19 (87:15)** — "fa-ṣallā" — THE BRIDGE VERB. The root OBJECT-FREE, human-ACTIVE: "mentioned the Name of his Lord THEN STOOD." The SUCCESS-TRIPLET (purify + mention-the-Name + stand) attested in three suras (56, 74, 87). Declared ALREADY WRITTEN in the Scrolls of Abraham and Moses. fa-ṣallā (he stood, self-taken) vs yuṣlā (he is stood, A17, installed) = the two directions of one standing-state. **THE STUDY'S HINGE.**

**A20 (88:4)** — "tuṣlā nāran ḥāmiyah." Third of the PASSIVE-TRIO (84/87/88). The fire-stand is SERVED and FURNISHED (scalding-spring drink, dari-thorns food) vs the God-side's raised-beds/cups/cushions/carpets. The standing-state is FULL-BODY both directions.

**A21 (92:15)** — "lā yasla-ha illā al-ashqā." EXCLUSION-GRAMMAR: "no one s-l-w-s her except the most-wretched." The fire-stand is a CONFINED class. The double-verb enrollment (deny-and-turn, 92:16) completes the fire-people's identification. The keep-away grammar (92:17: God keeps the taqwa away) - both keepings are God's acts.

**A22 (111:3)** — "say-sla naran dhat lahab." The NAME-TAG PUN: Abu-Lahab ("father of flame") will be stood into a fire "possessing flame." The fire-stand is name-signed: he will stand inside the fire of his own name. The wealth-thread closed its circle (A1 swallowed-fire → A22 hands-perished).

## Part 3 — Cluster B: Divine ṢALĀH (STATE pole, God-side, 7 verses)

The divine ṢALĀH is the OPPOSITE pole of the fire-stand: imposed-standing (fire) vs granted-standing (God's ṢALĀH). The ṢALĀH-chain: human act (ṣalāh/spending/repentance) → divine ṢALĀH (with angels) → exodus (darkness→light) → SALĀM-greeting.

**B1 (24:41)** — "the birds with wings spread; each one has known its ṢALĀH and its tasbīḥ." The STRONGEST "state not act" proof: the birds KNOW their ṣalāh by creation. The ṢALĀH is a CREATED POSTURE. The maṣīr-word serves both poles (fire-destination vs God-destination).

**B2 (2:157)** — "upon them are ṢALAWĀT from their Lord and mercy." FIRST divine ṢALĀH-verse: ṢALAWĀT GRANTED (not imposed) to those who CONFESS return ("we belong to God and to Him we return"). Paired with MERCY (raḥmah). Human ṣalāh (2:153) is the request; divine ṢALAWĀT (2:157) is the answer. First confirmation of the A19 bridge.

**B3 (33:43)** — "He is the One who s-l-l on you, and His angels, that He may bring you out of darkness into light." The ANGELIC pole: angels serve BOTH sides (fire-guard 74:30-31 and God-ṢALĀH 33:43). The EXODUS-clause: brings OUT of darkness INTO light - OPPOSITE of fire-delivery. Outcome = SALĀM-greeting (33:44). Second confirmation of A19 bridge.

**B4 (33:56)** — "God and His angels s-l-l on the Prophet. O you who believe, s-l-l on him and surrender a surrendering." The COMMAND-verse: humans commanded to ṢALLŪ ʿALAYHI (stand-toward the Prophet) AND SALLIMŪ TASLĪMAN (surrender). The s-l-w + s-l-m pairing. Divine ṢALĀH has TWO objects: Prophet (B4) and believers (B3). Negative = la'nah (curse) on harmers (33:57).

**B5 (9:103)** — "take charity that purifies them and cleanses them; and s-l-l on them; your ṣalāh is their TRANQUILITY (sakan)." The SAKAN-lexeme: the ṢALĀH as a SETTLING-OF-TRANQUILITY (positive counterpart of fire's settling-of-HOLDING: qarār/mihād/ma'āb). Purification-then-ṢALĀH chain.

**B6 (9:99)** — "ṣalawāt of the Messenger." The ṢALĀH-as-CURRENCY: spending counted as ṢALAWĀT of the Messenger. The ṢALĀH can be EARNED (by standing 2:153, by spending 9:99, by repentance 9:103). Three human inputs to the ṢALĀH-chain. The Messenger is the CHANNEL.

**B7 (9:84)** — "do NOT s-l-l on any of them who die, EVER, and do NOT stand at his grave." The NEGATIVE-command: the ṢALĀH is HONOR that can be WITHHELD. The honor-economy's sharpest form. The SAME passage contains both: disbelievers denied ṢALĀH (9:84) and believers earning ṢALĀH (9:99).

## Part 4 — Cluster C: The Commanded Formula (STATE pole, human-side, 21 verses)

The command-side's ṢALĀH: ESTABLISHED (yuqīmū - make-stand the standing-state) in SEVEN forms: physical (standing, C13), temporal (timed, C14), vocal (moderated voice, C15), preservative (guarded, C16), personal (commanded to the self, C19), purifying (purification-package, C20), eased (adjusted to capacity, C21).

**C1 (98:5)** — "establish the ṢALĀH and give the zakah" = "the religion of uprightness (dīn al-qayyimah)." THE MINIMAL RELIGION: worship God purely + upright + establish ṢALĀH + give zakah. The ṢALĀH is ESTABLISHED (yuqīmū = make-stand), not performed. The ṢALĀH is the DIVIDER between "worst of creatures" (98:6, Jahannam) and "best of creatures" (98:7, gardens).

**C2 (2:43)** — "establish the ṢALĀH and give the zakah and BOW WITH THOSE WHO BOW." Community-dimension added. The ṢALĀH is HEAVY except for the humble (2:45); God is with the patient (2:153). The ṢALĀH is for those who orient toward MEETING and RETURNING (2:46).

**C3 (2:83)** — "establish the ṢALĀH and give the zakah; THEN YOU TURNED AWAY." The ṢALĀH is embedded in an 8-item SOCIAL-ETHICAL covenant. The covenant-breakers TURNED AWAY (tawallaytum) - the SAME verb as the fire-people's self-steering (A4, A21). The ṢALĀH is the act that is TURNED AWAY from.

**C4 (5:12)** — "I am with you IF you establish the ṢALĀH and give the zakah and believe in My messengers and support them and lend God a good loan." The ṢALĀH is condition #1 of a FIVE-condition covenant. The breaking leads to the CURSE (la'nah, 5:13).

**C5 (2:125)** — "take from the standing-place of Abraham a MUSSALLĀ." The SITE-lexeme: the believer's standing-site is a SINGLE assigned spot, inherited from Abraham's MAQĀM. Paired with: the IMAMATE, the COVENANT of PURIFICATION, the SUBMISSION, the RITES, the MESSENGER, the UPRIGHT RELIGION, and the SCROLLS.

**C6 (14:37)** — "so that they may ESTABLISH THE SALAH." The ṢALĀH is the PURPOSE of settlement: Abraham settles his offspring near the House SO THAT they may ESTABLISH THE SALAH. The site exists FOR the act.

**C7 (14:40)** — "make me an ESTABLISHER OF THE SALAH, and from my offspring." The ṢALĀH-establishment is a GIFT-PRAYER: God makes the establisher. The ṢALĀH is a HERITAGE (passed from father to offspring). For the Day of Reckoning (Qiyāmah).

**C8 (19:31)** — "He enjoined on me the ṢALĀH and the ZAKAH as long as I live." The ṢALĀH is a PROPHETIC ENJOINMENT (awṣā) and a LIFETIME commitment. The ṢALĀH produces SALĀM (19:33). The ṢALĀH is the straight path (19:36).

**C9 (19:55)** — "he used to COMMAND his family the ṢALĀH and the ZAKAH, and he was pleasing to his Lord." Isma'il COMMANDED (yaʾmuru) his family the ṢALĀH. The ṢALĀH is the act of pleasing God. And 19:59: "they LOST THE SALAH and followed desires" → the SURAH-19 BRIDGE (A7's golden bridge).

**C10 (20:14)** — "establish the ṢALĀH FOR MY REMEMBRANCE." The ṢALĀH's PURPOSE is remembrance (dhikr). The fire-lexicon's deepest polarity: fire as delivery (A) vs fire as guidance (the burning bush gives the ṢALĀH-command). The ṢALĀH is given in a SACRED PLACE (Tuwa) to a CHOSEN one.

**C11 (11:87)** — "Does YOUR SALAH command you?" The MOCKERS' VIEW: they personify the ṢALĀH as a COMMANDING-FORCE. Even the mockers see the ṢALĀH as an active power that reforms worship AND commerce.

**C12 (6:162)** — "my ṢALĀH and my sacrifice and my life and my death are for God." The FOUR-FOLD DEDICATION: standing + sacrifice + living + dying, all for God. The ṢALĀH is the UNDIVIDED religion (6:159).

**C13 (3:39)** — "Zakariyya standing-praying (qāʾim yuṣallī) in the miḥrāb." The ṢALĀH's PHYSICAL FORM: STANDING (qāʾim) in a SANCTUARY (miḥrāb). The divine response (angelic announcement of Yahya) came DURING the ṢALĀH. The ṢALĀH-site is a place of PROVISION (rizq).

**C14 (17:78)** — "establish the ṢALĀH at the decline of the sun until the darkness of the night, and the Quran of dawn; indeed the Quran of dawn is WITNESSED." The ṢALĀH's TEMPORAL FORM: timed boundaries. The dawn ṢALĀH is WITNESSED by angels. The night-prayer (nāfilah) earns a praised station (maqām maḥmūd).

**C15 (17:110)** — "do not make your voice too loud in your ṢALĀH, nor too quiet, but seek a path between them." The ṢALĀH's VOCAL FORM: a MODERATED voice. The ṢALĀH includes Quran-recitation (which causes prostration and weeping), glorification (tasbīḥ), and magnification (takbīr).

**C16 (2:238)** — "guard the ṢALAWAT and the middle ṢALĀH; stand for God in devout obedience." The ṢALĀH's GUARDING-DIMENSION: the ṢALĀH is a TRUST to be preserved. The ṢALĀH has a RANKING (middle vs others). The ṢALĀH is ADAPTABLE (on foot or riding).

**C17 (11:114)** — "establish the ṢALĀH at the two ends of the day and at the approach of the night. Indeed the good deeds REMOVE the evil deeds." The ṢALĀH's SIN-REMOVAL: the good deeds remove the evil deeds. The ṢALĀH has THREE time-slots (morning + evening + approach-of-night).

**C18 (20:132)** — "command your family the ṢALĀH and be steadfast upon it." The ṢALĀH as a FAMILY COMMAND requiring STEADFASTNESS. The outcome is for taqwa (righteousness). The ṢALĀH is not an economic transaction ("We do not ask you for provision").

**C19 (31:17)** — "establish the ṢALĀH, enjoin right, forbid wrong, be patient; that is of the matters of determination." The ṢALĀH is command #1 of a four-command personal formula. The ṢALĀH is a matter of DETERMINATION (ʿazm). The ṢALĀH is the alternative to shirk (31:13).

**C20 (33:33)** — "establish the ṢALĀH and give the zakah and obey God and His Messenger. God only desires to remove from you the impurity and to purify you with a thorough purification." The ṢALĀH as a PURIFYING act: the ṢALĀH removes impurity (rijs) and purifies (taṭhīr). The ṢALĀH + ZAKAH + OBEDIENCE formula.

**C21 (73:20)** — "recite what is easy of the Quran... establish the ṢALĀH and give the zakah and lend God a good loan." The ṢALĀH's EASED ARRANGEMENT: God turns in mercy and eases the arrangement. The ṢALĀH is for ALL states (sick, traveling, fighting). The ṢALĀH + ZAKAH + QARḌ is the three-pillar formula.

## Part 5 — Cluster D: Life-Institutions (44 verses)

The universal formula + life-institutions cluster: war (4:101-103), Friday (62:9-10), washing (5:6), witnesses (5:106), debt-deferred prayer (4:43), travel-shortening (4:101), ṢALĀH as clock of life (24:58), etc.

**D1 (2:3)** — "those who believe in the unseen and establish the ṢALĀH and spend from what We have provided them." The ṢALĀH + SPENDING pair = the TWO-act definition of the believer. The ṢALĀH is the act of the UNSEEN-believer. The ṢALĀH is the proof of belief (2:8).

**D2 (2:110)** — "establish the ṢALĀH and give the zakah. Whatever good you send forth, you will find it with God." The ṢALĀH is an INVESTMENT: you send it forth, and you will find it with God. God SEES the ṢALĀH.

**D3 (2:177)** — "Righteousness (birr) is... one who believes in God and the Last Day and the angels and the Book and the prophets, and gives wealth... AND ESTABLISHES THE SALAH AND GIVES THE ZAKAH... and fulfills promises and is patient in poverty, hardship, and battle." The ṢALĀH is ONE element of a 10-item RIGHTEOUSNESS-CHARACTER. The ṢALĀH is the marker of truthfulness and righteousness.

**D4 (2:277)** — "those who believe and do righteous deeds and establish the ṢALĀH and give the zakah: for them is their reward with their Lord, and NO FEAR shall be on them, NOR SHALL THEY GRIEVE." The ṢALĀH is the ANTI-USURY act: standing-toward-God vs consuming-riba. The ṢALĀH brings FEARLESSNESS and GRIEFLESSNESS.

**D5 (2:45)** — "seek help in patience and the ṢALĀH; it is indeed HEAVY except upon the HUMBLE." The ṢALĀH's WEIGHT-DIMENSION: heavy for the proud, light for the humble. The ṢALĀH is paired with PATIENCE.

**D6 (2:153)** — "seek help in patience and the ṢALĀH; God is with the PATIENT." The DIVINE PRESENCE: God is with the patient. The ṢALĀH is for those willing to die for God (2:154).

**D7 (4:43)** — "do not APPROACH THE SALAH while intoxicated or in major impurity... if no water, use clean earth (tayammum)." The ṢALĀH's PURITY-GATE: requires washing (wuḍūʾ) or tayammum. The ṢALĀH is both RIGID (requires purity) and FLEXIBLE (adapts to water availability). God is PARDONING and FORGIVING.

**D8 (4:77)** — "restrain your hands and establish the ṢALĀH and give the zakah. Then when fighting was prescribed for them, a group of them fear people as they should fear God." The ṢALĀH as the PEACE-ACT: established in peace, not in war. The ṢALĀH is for one who FEARS GOD, not people.

**D9-D11 (4:101-103)** — "shorten the ṢALĀH in travel/fear... establish the ṢALĀH in shifts during war... remember God standing and sitting and on your sides... the ṢALĀH was upon the believers a PRESCRIBED WRIT (kitāban mawqūtan)." The ṢALĀH's ADAPTABILITY and LEGALITY: shortened in fear, performed in shifts during war, adapted to rain/sickness, and a PRESCRIBED WRIT with fixed times.

**D12 (4:162)** — "the firm in knowledge... and the establishers of the ṢALĀH and the givers of the zakah... to those We will give a great reward." The ṢALĀH-establishers are a DISTINCT CATEGORY among the believers, paired with KNOWLEDGE (rāsikhūna fī al-ʿilmi).

**D13 (5:6)** — "when you RISE TO THE SALAH, then WASH your faces and your hands up to the elbows, and WIPE your heads and your feet." The ṢALĀH's WASHING-DIMENSION (wuḍūʾ). The ṢALĀH is a FULL-BODY act: the body must be washed before standing. God is PARDONING and FORGIVING.

**D14 (5:55)** — "your ally is only God and His Messenger and those who believe - those who establish the ṢALĀH and give the zakah WHILE THEY BOW (rākiʿūn)." The ṢALĀH's BOWING-DIMENSION: the ṢALĀH is standing-AND-bowing. The ṢALĀH-establishers are God's ALLIES and the PARTY OF GOD (victorious).

## Part 6 — Cluster E: The Transformative Check (13 verses)

The E-cluster tests whether the ṢALĀH transforms: the ṢALĀH PREVENTS from immorality and wrongdoing (29:45). The ṢALĀH is both the act of the transformed (humble, constant, guarded, preventive, forgiven) and the act of the untransformed (hypocrite, rejector, mocker, persecuted).

**E1 (2:45)** — "seek help in patience and the ṢALĀH; it is indeed HEAVY except upon the HUMBLE." The ṢALĀH's WEIGHT: heavy for the proud, light for the humble.

**E2 (2:153)** — "seek help in patience and the ṢALĀH; God is with the PATIENT." The DIVINE PRESENCE: God is with the patient. The ṢALĀH is for those willing to die for God.

**E3 (4:142)** — "when they rise to the ṢALĀH, they rise LAZILY, showing off to the people, and they do not remember God except a little." The ṢALĀH of the HYPOCRITE: lazy, show-off, little-rememberer. The ṢALĀH is the act of the deceived.

**E4 (8:35)** — "their ṢALĀH at the House was not but whistling and hand-clapping." The ṢALĀH of the MOCKER: whistling and clapping. The ṢALĀH is the act of the House's ally (the righteous) vs the mocker.

**E5 (19:59)** — "they LOST THE SALAH and followed desires." THE SURAH-19 BRIDGE: ṢALĀH-loss → desires → fire-delivery. The ṢALĀH is what is LOST by the successor.

**E6 (23:2)** — "those who are HUMBLE in their ṢALĀH." The ṢALĀH's HUMILITY-DIMENSION: the ṢALĀH produces humility (khushūʿ). The ṢALĀH-humble are the INHERITORS of Firdaws.

**E7 (23:9)** — "those who GUARD their ṢALAWĀT." The ṢALAWĀT (renewed cycles) are GUARDED. The ṢALĀH-humble (E6) are the ṢALĀH-guarders (E7).

**E8 (29:45)** — "the SALAH PREVENTS from immorality and wrongdoing. And the remembrance of God is greater." THE TRANSFORMATIVE CHECK: the ṢALĀH PREVENTS (tanha) from immorality (fahsha') and wrongdoing (munkar). The ṢALĀH is the TRANSFORMATIVE act. The remembrance of God is GREATER than the prevention.

**E9+E10 (70:22-23, 70:34)** — "EXCEPT THE PRAYERS, those who are CONSTANT in their prayer... those who GUARD their ṢALAWĀT." The ṢALĀH is the act of the CONSTANT (not anxious, not impatient, not withholding). The ṢALĀH is both constant (70:23) and guarded (70:34). The ṢALĀH-constant-guarders inherit gardens (70:35).

**E11 (75:31)** — "He NEITHER BELIEVED nor PRAYED (ṣallā)." The ṢALĀH is the act of the BELIEVER: the rejector neither believes nor prays. The rejector DENIES and TURNS AWAY (75:32) - the same double-act as the fire-people.

**E12 (96:9-10)** — "Have you seen the one who forbids a servant when he prays?" The ṢALĀH is the act that is PERSECUTED. The ṢALĀH is the act of PROSTRATION and DRAWING NEAR (96:19 "prostrate and draw near"). The persecutor DENIES and TURNS AWAY (96:13).

**E13 (107:4-7)** — "Woe to those who pray, those who are HEEDLESS of their prayer, those who make SHOW, and withhold small kindnesses." The ṢALĀH is the act of the HYPOCRITE: heedless, show-off, withholder. The ṢALĀH is the act of the social-failure (drives away orphans, does not feed the poor). The ṢALĀH earns WOE when done hypocritically.

## Part 18 — The Settled Meaning

The root Ṣ-L-W (ص-ل-و) names a **standing-state within the full working reach of a power that determines one** — a sustained, renewed, settled exposure, not a momentary act.

**The SAME stem, TWO directions:**
- **Toward God (voluntary):** fa-ṣallā (87:15) — the human stands toward the Mentioned Name, after purification and Name-mention. The divine ṢALĀH (B-cluster) is God's RESPONSE: He s-l-l on the stander, bringing him out of darkness into light, with angels as co-agents, ending in SALĀM-greeting.
- **Toward fire (imposed):** y-u-ṣlā (84:12, 87:12, 88:4, 111:3) — the person is STOOD by the fire, after his own deny-and-turn (4:115, 58:8, 92:16). The fire-ṣalāh is the self-settlement of self-steering.

**The noun ṣalāh** = the STATE pole, willingly kept: a sustained, renewed standing-toward-God, qualified by soft-qualities (mercy, tranquility, peace, surrender, humility, constancy, prevention, sin-removal). The ṢALĀH is both what you ARE (a standing-state) and what you ARE IN (a standing-state within the reach of God).

**The fire-ṣalāh** = the DELIVERY pole, imposed: a sustained, renewed standing-within-fire, qualified by holding-qualities (qarār, mihād, ma'āb, maṣīr, ḥamim, sakan-of-holding). The fire-ṣalāh is both what you ARE (a stood-one) and what you ARE IN (a standing-state within the reach of fire).

**The site-words** = the SITE pole: space assigned to standing with the Name (ṢALAWĀT, MUSSALLĀ, MIḤRĀB).

## Conclusion

The Quran uses the root Ṣ-L-W (ص-ل-و) to name a **standing-state within the full working reach of a power that determines one** — sustained, renewed, settled. The same stem names:

- **The fire-stand** (DELIVERY pole): an imposed standing within the reach of fire, earned by self-steering, described with settlement-vocabulary (maṣīr, qarār, mihād, ma'āb) and holding-qualities (madhmum, madhur).
- **The divine ṢALĀH** (STATE pole, God-side): a granted standing within the reach of God, given as mercy/exodus, qualified by soft-qualities (raḥmah, sakan, SALĀM, taslīm, humility, constancy, prevention).
- **The commanded ṢALĀH** (STATE pole, human-side): an established standing within the reach of God, performed with physical (standing/bowing/prostration), temporal (timed), vocal (moderated voice), purifying (washed), and preservative (guarded) forms.
- **The standing-sites** (SITE pole): space assigned to standing with the Name (MUSSALLĀ, MIḤRĀB, ṢALAWĀT).

The ṢALĀH is both what you ARE (a standing-state) and what you ARE IN (a standing-state within the reach of a power). The fire-ṣalāh is the same structure, imposed. The root's native meaning is **to stand within the reach of a power** — and the Quran builds its entire theology of worship, judgment, and transformation on this single standing-state.
