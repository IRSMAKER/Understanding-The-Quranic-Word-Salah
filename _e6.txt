## E6 - 23:2 - alladhina hum fi salatihim khashi'un

ARABIC (flow): 23:1 "Successful are the believers" -> **23:2 "Those who are HUMBLE (khāshiʿūn) in their SALAH"** -> 23:3 "And those who turn away from idle talk (laghw)" -> 23:4 "And those who are doers of the zakah" -> 23:5-7 "And those who guard their private parts, except from their wives or what their right hands possess..." -> 23:8 "And those who are faithful to their trusts and their covenants" -> 23:9 "And those who GUARD their SALAWAT (plural)" -> 23:10-11 "Those are the inheritors who will inherit the Firdaws; they will abide therein eternally."

WORD FORM: khāshiʿūn (humble, active participle) + fī (in) ṣalātihim (their ṣalāh) - the ṢALĀH is the act of the HUMBLE: "those who are humble IN their ṢALĀH." The ṢALĀH is the act that PRODUCES humility (khushūʿ).

CONTEXT: MAKAN, Surah al-Mu'minun (23). The verse sits in the success-passage (23:1-11): successful are the believers (23:1), those who are humble in their ṢALĀH (23:2), those who turn away from idle talk (23:3), those who are doers of the zakah (23:4), those who guard their private parts (23:5-7), those who are faithful to trusts and covenants (23:8), those who guard their ṢALAWĀT (23:9), those are the inheritors of Firdaws (23:10-11).

GRAMMAR & LEXICON:
- THE HUMILITY (khushūʿ): "Those who are HUMBLE (khāshiʿūn) in their ṢALĀH." The ṢALĀH is the act of the HUMBLE: the ṢALĀH produces humility (khushūʿ). The ṢALĀH is the act of one who is humble in it. The same as D5 (2:45 "heavy except on the humble") and D15 (17:109 "it increases them in humility"). The ṢALĀH and humility are paired: the ṢALĀH produces humility, and the humble find the ṢALĀH light.
- THE IDLE-TALK-AVOIDANCE (23:3 "those who turn away from idle talk (laghw)"): the ṢALĀH is the act of one who turns away from idle talk. The ṢALĀH is the anti-laghv act. The same as D16 (5:91 - Satan averts from the ṢALĀH through intoxicants and gambling) and D34 (24:37 - "neither trade nor sale distracts them"). The ṢALĀH is the act that opposes idle talk, trade, and Satan's aversion.
- THE ṢALĀH + ZAKAH PAIR (23:4 "those who are doers of the zakah"): the SAME two-pillar formula (ṢALĀH + ZAKAH) as C1 (98:5), C2 (2:43), etc. The ṢALĀH and the zakah are the TWO acts of the successful.
- THE TRUST-FAITHFULNESS (23:8 "those who are faithful to their trusts and their covenants"): the ṢALĀH is the act of one who is faithful to trusts and covenants. The ṢALĀH is the act of the faithful. The same as C3 (2:83 - "establish the ṢALĀH and give the zakah; then you turned away") - the ṢALĀH is the act of one who does NOT turn away.
- 23:10-11 "Those are the inheritors who will inherit the Firdaws; they will abide therein eternally" - the ṢALĀH-humble are the INHERITORS of Firdaws (the highest garden). The ṢALĀH is the act of one who inherits Firdaws.

PARALLELS:
- D5 (2:45 - "heavy except on the humble"): D5 gave the ṢALĀH's WEIGHT (heavy except for the humble). E6 gives the ṢALĀH's HUMILITY (those who are humble in their ṢALĀH). The ṢALĀH is heavy (D5) and produces humility (E6). The weight and the humility are paired: the ṢALĀH is heavy for the proud, but the humble find it light, and the ṢALĀH produces more humility.
- D15 (17:109 - "it increases them in humility"): E6 confirms: the ṢALĀH increases humility. The ṢALĀH is the act that PRODUCES humility (khushūʿ). The same as 23:2 ("humble in their ṢALĀH").
- A17 (84:6 - "toil toward your Lord and you will MEET Him"): the meet-word (mulāqāh) - the ṢALĀH is for those who orient toward MEETING. E6's humble ones are those who orient toward meeting God.
- B2 (2:157 - ṢALAWĀT upon the patient + raḥmah): the divine ṢALAWĀT is granted to those who are HUMBLE in their ṢALĀH (E6). The human ṢALĀH (humble) invites the divine ṢALAWĀT.
- 23:2 "those who are humble in their ṢALĀH" - the ṢALĀH is the act of the humble. The same as 2:45 ("heavy except on the humble") - the ṢALĀH is heavy for the proud, light for the humble, and produces humility in the humble.

FINDING: E6 gives the ṢALĀH's HUMILITY-DIMENSION: "Those who are HUMBLE (khāshiʿūn) in their ṢALĀH." The ṢALĀH is the act of the HUMBLE: the ṢALĀH produces humility (khushūʿ). The ṢALĀH is the act of one who is humble in it. And the ṢALĀH-humble are the INHERITORS of Firdaws (23:10-11). The ṢALĀH is the act of one who inherits the highest garden. The ṢALĀH is paired with: turning away from idle talk (23:3), doing zakah (23:4), guarding private parts (23:5-7), being faithful to trusts and covenants (23:8). The ṢALĀH is embedded in a comprehensive ethical character.
